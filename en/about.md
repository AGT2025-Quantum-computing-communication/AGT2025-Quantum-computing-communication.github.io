## Academic Background

I obtained my **PhD in Quantum Communication and Computing from the University of Dschang, Cameroon, in 2021**.

Following the completion of my doctoral studies, I undertook a four-month research visit at the **University of Paderborn, Germany**, where I had the opportunity to broaden my research experience in an international academic environment.

In October 2023, I began postdoctoral research at the **University of KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa**, further developing my research activities in quantum information science and computational methods.

These academic experiences have contributed significantly to my scientific development and strengthened my interest in international research collaboration.

## Research Experience

My research experience lies at the intersection of quantum computing, quantum communication, and computational physics.

I am particularly interested in the design and implementation of quantum algorithms, investigating their computational complexity, evaluating their performance through numerical simulations, and exploring their applicability to scientific problems.

Some of the topics I have investigated or explored include:

- Quantum algorithms for solving mathematical and scientific problems.
- Variational quantum algorithms and hybrid quantum-classical optimization.
- Quantum phase estimation and Hamiltonian-based computational methods.
- Quantum key distribution and quantum cryptographic protocols.
- Quantum machine learning and quantum neural networks.
- Quantum error correction and quantum information protection.

## Research Interests

### Quantum Computing and Algorithms

Quantum algorithm development, quantum optimization, variational quantum algorithms, quantum phase estimation, and the application of quantum computing to scientific problems.

### Quantum Communication and Cryptography

Quantum communication protocols, quantum teleportation, superdense coding, and quantum key distribution protocols such as BB84, B92, and E91.

### Quantum Machine Learning

Quantum machine learning, particularly quantum neural networks, quantum support vector machines, and hybrid quantum-classical approaches for optimization and learning tasks.

### Quantum Error Correction

Quantum error-correcting codes, quantum information protection, and techniques for improving the reliability of quantum computation in noisy environments.

### Computational Physics and Numerical Methods

Numerical analysis, mathematical modelling, scientific computing, and computational approaches for solving differential equations and other scientific problems.

## Scientific and Computational Expertise

My research activities involve mathematical analysis, programming, numerical simulation, and quantum software development.

- **Programming:** Python.
- **Quantum programming:** Qiskit and PennyLane.
- **Scientific computing:** NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, and SageMath.
- **Quantum hardware and simulation:** IBM Quantum.
- **Research tools:** LaTeX, Linux, and high-performance computing environments.

## Research Philosophy and Motivation

I believe that quantum technologies represent an important direction in the evolution of scientific computing and information processing.

My motivation is to contribute to the development of reliable, efficient, and practically relevant quantum computational methods. I am particularly interested in understanding both the theoretical potential and practical limitations of quantum algorithms, including their computational complexity, circuit requirements, sensitivity to noise, and performance on available quantum hardware.

I also value the interaction between theoretical research, numerical experimentation, and practical implementation as essential elements of scientific progress.

## Academic and Professional Objectives

My long-term objective is to contribute to the advancement of quantum information science through innovative research, scientific collaboration, and education.

I am particularly interested in:

- Developing efficient quantum algorithms for scientific and engineering applications.
- Investigating quantum optimization and quantum machine learning methods.
- Exploring new approaches to secure quantum communication.
- Contributing to research on practical quantum computing and quantum error correction.
- Collaborating with international research groups and academic institutions.
- Promoting quantum computing education and scientific knowledge sharing.

## Beyond Academic Research

In addition to my research activities, I am interested in scientific education and the dissemination of knowledge in emerging quantum technologies.

Through **AGT-QuanTech**, I aim to contribute to the development of educational resources, programming tutorials, and specialized learning opportunities in quantum communication and quantum computing.

My objective is to help students, researchers, and technology enthusiasts acquire the theoretical knowledge and practical skills needed to explore the rapidly evolving field of quantum technologies.
